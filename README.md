# Naruto: The Journey of a Ninja

## A. Introduction

**Naruto** is a Japanese anime series about a young ninja named Naruto Uzumaki. He dreams of becoming the Hokage, who is the leader of the Hidden Leaf Village. Even though many people dislike him, he continues to work hard and never gives up on his dream.

Naruto is an anime with **Action, Adventure, Fantasy, and Martial Arts** genres. It was first released in **2002** and was created by Masashi Kishimoto.

## B. Synopsis

The story is about Naruto Uzumaki, a young ninja who wants to become the Hokage someday. When he was born, a powerful Nine-Tailed Fox was sealed inside his body. Because of this, many people in his village avoid him and treat him differently.

Naruto later becomes part of Team 7 with Sasuke Uchiha and Sakura Haruno. Their teacher is Kakashi Hatake, who trains and guides them on their missions. As they go on different adventures, they meet strong enemies and face many challenges. Naruto also makes new friends and learns the importance of teamwork, friendship, and trust. Throughout the story, he continues to work hard and prove that he can become a great ninja.

> "Never give up on your dreams, no matter how difficult the journey becomes."

## C. Characters

Here are five important characters in the Naruto series:

1. *Naruto Uzumaki* – The main character who wants to become the Hokage. He is cheerful, hardworking, and determined.
2. *Sasuke Uchiha* – Naruto's teammate and rival. He is a talented ninja who wants to become stronger.
3. *Sakura Haruno* – A member of Team 7. She is smart, caring, and always tries to improve herself.
4. *Kakashi Hatake* – The leader and teacher of Team 7. He is a skilled ninja who guides Naruto, Sasuke, and Sakura.
5. *Iruka Umino* – Naruto's former teacher at the Ninja Academy. He cares about Naruto and supports him.

## D. Personal Review

I chose Naruto because it is one of my favorite anime series. I enjoy watching it because of its interesting story, characters, and exciting ninja battles. I also like how the anime teaches lessons that we can apply in our everyday lives.

### Three Things I Like About Naruto

- **The Story:** I like how the story shows Naruto's journey from being an ordinary student to becoming a stronger ninja.
- **The Characters:** Each character has a different personality, background, and ability, which makes the anime more interesting.
- **The Action Scenes:** I enjoy the ninja battles and the different techniques used by the characters.

### My Personal Rating

**9/10**

I highly recommend Naruto to people who enjoy action and adventure anime. It is fun to watch and has good lessons about friendship, hard work, and never giving up on your dreams.
